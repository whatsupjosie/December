# December workflow

Everything below in quotation marks is Josie's own wording, copied as it was relayed. Nothing in the quotes has been changed.

## The branches

- main
- main-assembly: "where we do the workshop work... incorporate them into what will eventually replace main"
- parts-assembly: "where we put all the parts that we haven't integrated yet... to get the parts in shape and make sure they work"
- 2i
- pubpartner
- iteration-wallet
- blackbox
- alex
- code-collector

About main and the program branches: "Main is for them all to be buddies and work together. Their own folders are for them, so they are separate individuals ready to go and do what we need without everyone else's baggage."

## Each part

"fixed up, proven to work, integrated into main assembly, and in the same session... that session isn't over until a handoff is written, documenting that the piece has been installed, and then the part itself is moved out of part assembly into parts archive."

## When main-assembly is ready

It "graduates and replaces main".

## The other repos

The storage repos are "the file pile". Parts-Live-Archive holds "the stars that made it out of the pile."
